# Frozen figure inputs

These compact tables are the final phenotype, genotype and significance inputs used by the manuscript renderers. They are retained in Git so figures can be drawn without maintaining every one-off candidate test. They are not source observations or an archive of full generated analyses.

- `candidate_disease_panels/`: JA/LysM final disease tests and eligible genotype calls.
- `chr2_leaf_water_figure/`: GDSL water, expression and disease-panel inputs.
- `hotspot_score_stability/`: saved significance annotations for the hotspot stability supplement.
- `loci/`: original gloss/LysM phenotype and allele tables needed during figure export.

Reproducible regional GWAS, gene models and LD outputs belong in ignored `data/generatable/loci/`. Main dataset recipes are in `data/generatable/README.md`. The frozen tables here are deliberately not regenerated by those recipes. Their original calculations are preserved under `deprecated/`.

- `illustration_sources.csv`: original image selections for manually assembled figure illustrations, with paths into the external image dataset.
